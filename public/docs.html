Introduction

Pinbox is a privacy-first temporary email service. It lets you generate instant disposable email addresses to receive messages, extract OTPs, and resolve magic links, all without giving away your real email address.

Pinbox is ideal for signing up to new services, verifying accounts, receiving one-time codes, and anything else you'd rather not associate with your personal inbox.

Generate an Email

1

Click "Generate Email Address"

On the Pinbox app screen, tap the primary button to instantly generate a disposable email address at the aiplex.app domain.

2

Copy your address

Once generated, your email address is displayed prominently. Click "Copy" to copy it to your clipboard instantly.

3

Use it anywhere

Paste the address into any signup form. Pinbox will receive emails sent to it in real time.

Using the Inbox

Once an email is active, new messages appear in your inbox instantly as they arrive.

Tap any email in the list to open the full message, including the full message with images and formatting.

OTP Extraction

Pinbox automatically scans incoming emails for verification codes using smart scanning. When detected, the code is displayed in a prominent banner at the top of the screen and can be copied with one tap.

Magic Link Resolution

Some services send a "magic link" instead of a numeric code a URL you click to verify your account. Pinbox detects these links and opens them safely for you, automatically finding any verification codes inside.

This works for applications,live websites,ai services and others that use link-based authentication.

QR Code

Every temporary email address you generate comes with a unique QR code. This makes it easy to share your temporary email with other devices or use it across platforms.

How to use QR codes:

  • Scan with your phone Use any QR scanner app to quickly copy the email address to your mobile device
  • Share with others Let team members use the same temporary email for testing purposes
  • Cross-device workflow Generate an email on desktop, scan the QR code, and use it on your mobile device

The QR code appears automatically when you generate a new email address. Simply click it to view it in full screen for easy scanning.

History

Pinbox keeps track of all the temporary email addresses you've generated during your current session. This makes it easy to switch between multiple addresses or review past ones.

Using History:

  • View past addresses See all email addresses you've created
  • Quick switch Click any address in history to switch back to it instantly
  • Track usage See which services you've signed up for and when

Your history is stored locally in your browser and is cleared when you log out. For privacy reasons, we don't store this information on our servers.

Disposable email addresses are stored locally in your browser in case you lose access after using one for something important, please contact support@aiplex.app for recovery assistance.

Authentication

Getting started with Pinbox is easy. You have two options to create an account:

Sign up with Username & Password

  • Choose a unique username
  • Create a strong password
  • Verify your account

Sign in with Google

  • One-click sign in using your Google account
  • No need to remember another password
  • Your Google credentials are verified securely

Your account helps you keep track of how many emails you've generated, manage your subscription plan, and access your usage history. For your safety, you'll be automatically logged out after a period of inactivity.
